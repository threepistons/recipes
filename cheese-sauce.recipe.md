# Cheese sauce

Adapted from Mrs Beeton's cornflour sauce and a Doves Farm recipe that has disappeared from their website.

This is a dish component, you don't eat it on its own but use it as part of something else. Typical use cases include:

 * acting as the milk sauce layer in a lasagne;
 * pouring over cauliflower or pasta to make cauliflower or macaroni cheese;
 * when made with blue stilton, served as the "blue cheese sauce" with a steak.
 
The amount below makes enough cheese sauce for three-to-four people to have macaroni cheese.

### Ingredients

 * 300 ml milk, in a measuring vessel
 * 125 g cheese, in small pieces. I suggest a mature coloured cheddar for most purposes, the colour makes it easier to see when the sauce is uniform.
 * 20 g corn flour
 * 1/2 tsp mustard powder, optional
 * 1 tbsp worcester sauce, optional
 * 1/2 tsp paprika
 * 1 to_taste salt and pepper

### Directions

1. Put all the milk bar 50ml into the pan and start heating it.
2. Mix the corn flour with the dry seasonings and add to the 50ml of milk still in the measuring vessel. Stir until the mixture is uniform and lump-free.
3. When the milk in the pan starts to boil, remove the pan from the heat.
4. Slowly add the corn flour mixture into the pan of hot milk, stirring continuously.
5. Return the pan to the heat and stir continuously whilst the sauce thickens.
6. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the cheese until the sauce is uniform in appearance and add the worcester sauce.  You must not boil the sauce once you start adding cheese.
